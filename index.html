// about

About

I joined Rapid7 as an intern in 2015 and have spent the decade since in vulnerability detection, moving from Java engineer to tech lead to Engineering Manager.

Since August 2024 I've led engineering teams across Belfast, Toronto and Pune, with two Engineering Managers reporting to me. My teams own vulnerability signatures and detections, and the big data analytics behind them.

200+ SOURCES

Vulnerability data at scale

Teams ingest third-party vulnerability data from 200+ upstream sources, from structured feeds like NVD to scraped vendor advisories, and normalise it into a standard schema that powers detections.

A pipeline on AWS Step Functions, EventBridge and Lambda moves 3M+ raw metadata files into a Snowflake data lake.
